The Box

Keep it close.
Keep it quiet.

Keyless cars can be opened by relaying the signal from a key left in the hallway. Our box is lined with signal-blocking fabric, so your keys stay silent until you need them.

Test it in 30 seconds

Don't take our word for it.

Put your car key inside, close the lid fully and fasten the latch.

Walk to your car with the box closed.

Try the door handle. It should stay locked. Take the key out and it works again.

Results can vary by key type and vehicle. If your car still unlocks, check the lid is fully closed and test again. We recommend re-testing every few months. A signal-blocking box reduces the risk of relay theft but can't guarantee your car won't be stolen.
